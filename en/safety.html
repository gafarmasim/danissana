Safety Tips

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Protect your account

  • Choose a strong password and never share it. The Danışsana team will never ask for your password or codes.
  • Keep your recovery code somewhere safe — it is the only way to reset a forgotten password.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication: Settings (Ayarlar) › Account and security (Hesab və təhlükəsizlik) › Two-step verification (İki mərhələli təsdiq).
  • If you see an unknown device — Settings (Ayarlar) › Account and security (Hesab və təhlükəsizlik) › Active devices (Aktiv cihazlar) → sign it out and change your password.

In random chat

  • The other person only sees your alias; only you can reveal your name with “Show myself” (Özümü göstər).
  • Don’t share your phone number, address, workplace or social media accounts.
  • Voice chat only starts when both of you agree and can be ended at any time. Voice is not recorded.
  • If you don’t like it — “End” (Bitir), 👎 in the rating (you won’t be matched again) or report.

Avoid scams

  • Don’t trust anyone asking for money, card details, SMS codes or passwords.
  • “Send me a gift and I’ll send you more back” is a scam.
  • Coins are only bought in the App through the App Store. Buying or selling coins, accounts or coupons outside the App is prohibited and unsafe.

Meeting in person

If you meet someone you got to know online — meet in a public place, tell someone close to you, and use your own transport.

Who can message and call you

Settings (Ayarlar) › Privacy (Məxfilik) — “Who can message me”, “Who can call me”, “Blocked users”.

If you need help

  • Disturbing behaviour — “Report” (Şikayət et) and “Block” (Blokla) on their profile.
  • Immediate danger — 112.
