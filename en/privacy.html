Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Danışsana mobile app (the “App”) collects about you, why, and how it is protected. The App is provided by Gafar Masimli. Contact: gafardeveloper1@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account

  • Username and password. The password is stored only as a hash — we cannot see it.
  • Date of birth — only to verify that you are 18 or older; it is not shown on your profile.

Profile (optional)

  • Name, bio, city (typed by you), interests, languages, gender (can be hidden), profile photo, voice bio.

Content you create

  • Rooms (title, cover image), room chat, direct messages (text and voice messages), clubs, gifts, follows.
  • Reports and appeals (including messages attached as evidence), support conversations and images.
  • Random chat messages and questions you post in room games.

Voice

  • Voice in rooms, calls and random chat is streamed live and is not recorded. Only durations (for example, call length) are stored.
  • Voice messages and voice bios are stored as files because you send them.

Identity verification (optional)

  • For the blue badge or paid features: a photo of your ID document (passport) and a selfie — used only for the check and deleted after the decision (within 7 days at most).
  • If you enter a personal ID number, it is stored only as an irreversible hash to prevent a second account with the same document.

Payments

  • Coin purchases go through the Apple App Store. We never see your card details.
  • We store the transaction number, product, date, wallet balances and transactions (coins, diamonds, coupons, VIP).

Device and security

  • Device model, operating system, app version, installation key (random code).
  • IP address and device information — for sign-ins, active devices and abuse protection.
  • Push notification token, last activity time.

Settings

  • Notification choices, quiet hours, privacy choices, consent to promotional notifications.
We do not collect: precise location (GPS), contacts or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party tracking for advertising or analytics, and we do not sell your data.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the service: rooms, messages, calls, notifications, wallet.
  • Account security: sign-ins, two-factor authentication, limits against fake accounts and request floods.
  • Moderation: reviewing reports and enforcing the rules.
  • Verifying payments and handling refunded purchases.
  • Support and legal obligations.
  • Promotional notifications — only with your consent; you can turn them off at any time.

3. Who can see it

  • Other users: your profile (according to your privacy settings) and what you say and write in rooms. Direct messages are visible only to the other person.
  • In random chat the other person sees only your alias unless you reveal yourself.
  • Moderators see reported content as evidence.
  • Administrators can view private conversations only with special permission (for example, a serious report or a legal request); every view is logged.
  • Service providers acting on our behalf: Supabase — database, files and server functions; LiveKit — live audio (EU, Frankfurt servers); Apple — payments and push notifications. Push notification text (sender name, start of the message) reaches your phone through Apple; message previews can be hidden in Settings (Ayarlar) › Notifications (Bildirişlər).
  • Authorities — only when required by law.

Data may be processed on servers outside Azerbaijan; transfers use encrypted connections.

4. How long we keep it

  • After you delete your account you can restore it within 30 days; then the account and its data are permanently deleted (except payment records we are legally required to keep). Messages you sent may remain in the other person’s conversation as “deleted account”.
  • ID document images — after the decision (within 7 days at most); unreviewed requests are closed after 30 days.
  • Random chat messages — deleted 30 days after the chat ends.
  • Support images — 180 days after the request is closed.
  • Sign-in history (IP, device) — 180 days.
  • Direct messages — until you clear the conversation or delete your account.

5. Your rights

  • Download your data: Settings (Ayarlar) › Privacy (Məxfilik) › Download my data (Məlumatlarımı yüklə).
  • Correct it: profile and settings.
  • Delete it: Settings (Ayarlar) › Account and security (Hesab və təhlükəsizlik) › Delete account (Hesabı sil) (or this page).
  • Control who can message or call you — Settings (Ayarlar) › Privacy (Məxfilik); you can block anyone.
  • Turn off notifications and promotions — Settings (Ayarlar) › Notifications (Bildirişlər).

Questions or complaints: gafardeveloper1@gmail.com.

6. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS / TLS). Passwords are stored as hashes and sign-in keys are stored encrypted on your phone. We use two-factor authentication, abuse limits and moderation. No system is perfectly secure, but we work continuously to reduce risk.

7. Age requirement

The App is only for users aged 18 and over. Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are closed.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, the date on this page is updated; for significant changes we notify you in the App.

9. Contact
