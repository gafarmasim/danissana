Community Guidelines

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Danışsana is a place for respectful and safe conversation. These rules apply everywhere: in rooms, messages, calls and random chat.

Do

  • Be respectful — disagreement is fine, insults are not.
  • Give others a chance to speak and follow the room host’s rules.
  • If something bothers you — report and block.

Don’t

  • Hate and discrimination based on nationality, religion, gender, disability, etc.
  • Threats, harassment, promotion of violence or self-harm.
  • Sexual or explicit content, sexual solicitation.
  • Anyone under 18 on the App; anything related to child exploitation — immediate ban and a report to the authorities.
  • Sharing someone else’s personal information (phone, address, photos) without permission; pressuring someone in random chat to reveal their identity.
  • Recording or sharing someone’s voice or conversation without permission.
  • Fraud, asking for money, fake accounts, impersonating others (including the Danışsana team).
  • Spam, advertising, illegal goods and services, drugs.

Hosts

A room host is responsible for keeping their room within the rules: they can mute speakers, move them off stage or remove them from the room.

If something is wrong

  • On a profile or in a message — “Report” (Şikayət et) and “Block” (Blokla).
  • In random chat — “End” and report; the latest messages are attached automatically as evidence.
  • If there is immediate danger, call local emergency services first.

Enforcement

Warning → temporary restriction → ban. Serious cases lead to an immediate ban. You can appeal decisions in the App.
