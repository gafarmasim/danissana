Account Deletion

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In the App

  1. Settings (Ayarlar) › Account and security (Hesab və təhlükəsizlik) › Delete account (Hesabı sil).
  2. Read what will be lost and confirm.
If you sign in again within 30 days, the account is restored. After that, it is permanently deleted.

What is deleted

  • Profile, photos, voice bio, followers and following, level and badges.
  • Coins, diamonds, coupon balance, VIP and shop items.
  • Messages you sent may remain in the other person’s conversation as “deleted account”.
  • Payment records that we are legally required to keep are kept until that period ends.

If you can’t sign in

Send a support request from the sign-in screen or email gafardeveloper1@gmail.com with your username. We may ask for additional information to confirm that you own the account.

Need a break instead?

Settings (Ayarlar) › Account and security (Hesab və təhlükəsizlik) › Temporarily freeze account (Hesabı müvəqqəti dondur). Signing in again reopens the account.
