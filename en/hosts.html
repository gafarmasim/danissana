Host Programme

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who can be a host

  • Anyone can open a room. To join the host programme, fill in the “Become a host” (Aparıcı ol) application in the App.
  • Our team reviews applications; the requirements (level, account age, no violations) are shown in the App.

What you earn

  • Diamonds from gifts received in your rooms.
  • A share of paid-room tickets — as diamonds (when the room runs according to the rules).
  • Task and monthly goal rewards.
  • Diamonds are an in-app unit — they convert to coins or coupon balance; there are no cash payouts in the App.

Obligations

  • Follow the Community Guidelines; a host is responsible for the rules in their room and can mute, move off stage or remove participants.
  • Fake activity, gifting yourself, account networks and prohibited “services” in exchange for gifts are not allowed.
  • Violations may lead to losing host status and earned rewards.

Agencies

Hosts can join an agency. The agency’s share and terms are agreed between the agency and the host; agencies must follow the rules too.

Verified host

Hosts who verify their identity and meet the requirements can get a blue badge: Settings (Ayarlar) › Account and security (Hesab və təhlükəsizlik) › Blue badge (Mavi nişan).
