Payments, Coins and Refunds

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Coins

  • Coins are the App’s virtual unit. They are sold in packs through the App Store; prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency. Card details are processed by Apple — we never see them.
  • Coins can be used to send gifts, buy VIP, shop items (frames, backgrounds, etc.) and tickets for paid rooms.

Free coins

The daily bonus, tasks, level rewards, inviting friends and promo codes give free coins. Free coins never become coupon balance.

Referral and promo codes

  • Within 7 days after sign-up you can enter one code — a friend’s referral code or a promo code.
  • A second account on the same device does not receive a bonus; suspicious cases are reviewed.
  • Bonus conditions are shown on the screen where you enter the code.

VIP

  • VIP is bought with coins for 30 days and does not renew automatically — we remind you 3 days before it ends.
  • On purchase, the tier’s monthly coins are returned to you immediately as free coins.
  • Buying the same tier again extends it by 30 days; upgrading converts the remaining days to the new tier.

Gifts and diamonds

  • Users who receive gifts earn diamonds. Diamonds can be converted to coins.
  • Diamonds that come from paid purchases can also be converted to coupon balance; the converted amount becomes available after 14 days. See Coupons.

Cash

Coins, diamonds, coupons and coupon balance cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another account (except by sending gifts or gifting coupons).

If a purchase doesn’t appear

Wallet (Cüzdan) › Coin packs (Coin paketləri) › “Restore purchases” (Alışları bərpa et). If that doesn’t help, email gafardeveloper1@gmail.com with the purchase date.

Refunds

  • App Store purchases are refunded by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • When Apple refunds a purchase, the coins from that purchase are deducted from your account.
  • If a paid room doesn’t run according to the rules (it was too short, you were removed from the room, the host was sanctioned, etc.), your ticket is refunded automatically.

When an account is deleted

Remaining coins, diamonds, coupon balance and VIP are lost and cannot be restored.
