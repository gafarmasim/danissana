Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password” (Parolu unutdum) and set a new password with your recovery code. If you don’t have a recovery code, send a support request from the same screen.

I lost the phone with two-factor authentication

Sign in with your recovery code (password and 2FA are reset) or contact support.

I bought coins but they don’t appear

Wallet (Cüzdan) › Coin packs (Coin paketləri) › “Restore purchases” (Alışları bərpa et). If that doesn’t help, email gafardeveloper1@gmail.com with the purchase date.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Someone is bothering me

Use “Report” (Şikayət et) and “Block” (Blokla) on their profile. Control who can message or call you in Settings (Ayarlar) › Privacy (Məxfilik).

I want to delete my account

See Account Deletion.
