Coupons and Partner Venues

Last updated: 9 October 2026

What is a coupon

A coupon is a discount at a partner venue (café, restaurant, etc.). Coupons are bought with coupon balance; coupon balance comes only from diamonds earned from paid purchases (bonus coins never become coupon balance).

How to get one

  1. Wallet (Cüzdan) › Coupons (Kuponlar) — choose a venue and an offer.
  2. Pay with coupon balance — the coupon appears in “My coupons” (Kuponlarım).

How to use it

  • At the till, open the coupon in “My coupons” (Kuponlarım) and show the code (QR); once the cashier confirms it, the coupon is used.
  • A coupon is valid for 30 days after purchase and can be used once.
  • Used coupons cannot be refunded.
  • You can gift a coupon to a friend — the code will then appear on their side.

Responsibility

Discount conditions are written on the coupon. The venue is responsible for its service, prices and quality. If there is a problem, email gafardeveloper1@gmail.com — we will resolve it together with the venue.

Want to become a partner?

If you own a venue: in the App, Wallet (Cüzdan) › Coupons (Kuponlar) › partner application, or email gafardeveloper1@gmail.com (subject: “Partner”).
