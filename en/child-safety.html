Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Danışsana has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Age requirement

  • The App is only for people aged 18 and over. Date of birth is checked at sign-up; anyone under 18 cannot register.
  • In suspicious cases we may require identity verification. Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are closed.

2. Prohibited

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — sharing, searching for, requesting or distributing it.
  • Sexual communication with minors, grooming, and content that sexualises children.
  • Any activity that promotes or facilitates the exploitation of children.

3. How we detect and act

  • Every profile, message, room and random chat has a “Report” (Şikayət et) option; the reasons “Under 18” (18 yaşdan kiçik) and “Inappropriate content” (Uyğunsuz məzmun) are reviewed with priority.
  • Moderators see reports together with evidence (recent messages).
  • When a violation is confirmed: the content is removed, the account is banned immediately and permanently, evidence is preserved as required by law and reported to the competent law-enforcement authorities.

4. How to report

  • In the App: “Report” (Şikayət et) on a profile, message or room → choose a reason.
  • Email: gafardeveloper1@gmail.com (subject: “Child safety”).
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 112 (Azerbaijan) or your local police right away.

5. Child safety point of contact

Gafar Masimli · gafardeveloper1@gmail.com

6. Compliance

We comply with the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan and applicable international child safety rules, and we cooperate with the competent authorities.
